# Suggested 90-second demo for judges (optional)

The initial 2026 NISR submission form does **not** ask for a video. This is only a suggested spoken walkthrough for a later review or coaching stage.

1. **Problem (15 sec):** “NISR annual Food Balance Sheets contain useful agricultural utilization and production data, but planning requires looking ahead. CropBalance Rwanda turns these national statistics into transparent scenarios.”
2. **Data (15 sec):** Open *Data & method*. “We used nine years, 15 key food commodities, and 135 reconciled commodity-year records, with source PDF page references.”
3. **National outlook (20 sec):** Open *National outlook*. Explain that negative differences mean domestic production below modeled utilization, **not automatically a food shortage**.
4. **Commodity demonstration (20 sec):** Open *Commodity explorer*, select rice or maize, and show the historical chart and model’s 2026 result. Change the production scenario in the sidebar.
5. **Integrity and impact (20 sec):** Open *Forecast checks*. “We benchmarked against a last-year baseline, which actually performed slightly better in our 2025 holdout; we disclose that limitation. This is a decision-support prototype for planners to investigate storage, trade, productivity and losses—not a claim of guaranteed AI accuracy.”

If asked why the app does not use a deep-learning model: nine annual observations per crop are insufficient to establish reliable deep-learning superiority. Our approach explicitly compares ML with simple statistical baselines and exposes limitations.
