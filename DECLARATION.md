**Originality Declaration**



We confirm that CropBalance Rwanda is our team's original project entry, developed around our idea of using agricultural data to understand crop demand and production in Rwanda. The project has not been submitted to any other competition or organization.



We acknowledge the sources and tools used in developing the project and take responsibility for the work submitted. We also understand and accept the competition's requirements and intellectual property terms.



**Our Contribution**



We developed the project idea and selected NISR's Food Balance Sheet data to examine agricultural production and domestic needs in Rwanda. Together, we discussed the problem, identified the main features of the application and considered how the findings could support better agricultural planning and decision-making.



**Data Sources and Tools**



Our project uses the following publications from the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR):



Rwanda Food Balance Sheets 2017–2021



Rwanda Food Balance Sheets 2022



Rwanda Food Balance Sheets 2023–2024



Rwanda Food Balance Sheets 2025



The application uses Python, Streamlit, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Altair and PyMuPDF.



Detailed information on the data sources and methodology is provided in docs/DATA\_SOURCES.md and docs/METHODOLOGY.md.
